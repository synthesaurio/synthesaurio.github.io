Events

Part of my artistic practice includes actively participate in creating spaces for community building. Here are some of the recent events I was involved with the organization.

Spiritual Exit tour, September 2025

Event part of Spiritual Exit tour (Portland, Oregon). The event had an emphasis on ambient/drone/experimental music. Other performers included: Sacramento artist Andrew Wayne and Synthesaurio (myself) performing an ambient set.

CEDA Album Release party, August 2025

Event for the release of the first part of my full length album. Some mutual aid groups were invited to event to share information about their work and ways to get involved. All sales of the tapes and merch was donated to humanitarian relief

May Day in community, May 2025

May day event with emphasis on mutual aid network building. Several mutual aid groups were invited to table and share resources about their work and ways to get involved. Local artists (Davis/Sac/Bay area) donated pieces for a raffle, where raffle tickets raised funds for the mutual aid organizations. Performances included 3 Davis bands and 1 from the Bay area.

Experimental Space, September 22, 2024 & February 2nd 2025

This was a series of event at third space Davis where music artists were paired with visuals creating an immersive experience.
